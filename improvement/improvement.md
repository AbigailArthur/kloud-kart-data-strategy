# Improvement Beyond the Brief: Monday Reporting Reconciliation and Sign-off Template

## The problem it solves

Kloud Kart's Operations and Finance teams report different figures, while Nana manually rebuilds the Monday management report. This creates extra work and makes it difficult to identify and resolve differences.

## What I built

I created a reusable Monday Reporting Reconciliation and Sign-off Template in Word.

The template provides sections for:
- Reporting period and preparation date.
- Sales, revenue and order figures from Operations and Finance.
- Comparison of reported figures and identification of differences.
- Explanations for identified differences.
- Assigned responsibility and follow-up actions.
- Operations and Finance review and sign-off.

The template uses blank fields and does not contain actual Kloud Kart business figures. It is a reporting aid, not an automated connection to the company's database.

## What it is worth to Kloud Kart

The template gives Operations and Finance a consistent structure for reviewing their figures before Nana prepares the Monday management report. It can reduce manual reconciliation work, make disagreements easier to investigate and improve accountability for resolving reporting differences.

## What it cost

The template was created using Microsoft Word and does not require a new server, paid hosting or additional software infrastructure. Staff time will be needed to complete the template, investigate differences and review the figures each week.
