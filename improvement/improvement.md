# Improvement beyond the brief: Customer Data Request and Deletion Tracker

## The problem it solves

Kloud Kart manages a marketing list containing approximately 300,000 people, but customer-data copies are not properly tracked.

The company also needs a reliable way to record customer-data requests, assign responsibility and retain evidence of actions taken.

Without a central tracking process, requests may be delayed or handled inconsistently. Staff may also struggle to establish which systems, exports and external processors have been checked.

## What I built

I created an Excel-based Customer Data Request and Deletion Tracker.

The workbook provides:

- A request register with unique request IDs.
- Fields for request dates, types, owners and statuses.
- Identity-verification tracking.
- A due-date field confirmed by the responsible privacy lead.
- Fields to record systems, exports and external processors checked.
- Space for actions, evidence references and retention exceptions.
- Automatic deadline-status indicators.
- Dropdown lists to support consistent data entry.
- Instructions on responsible use and data minimisation.

The workbook includes a fictional example for demonstration. It does not contain real customer information.

The tracker does not automatically locate or delete customer records. It supports record keeping and accountability but must be used alongside an approved privacy process.

## What it is worth to Kloud Kart

The tracker gives staff one place to monitor customer-data requests and see who is responsible for each one.

It can help the company:

- Identify requests that need attention.
- Monitor approaching deadlines.
- Record which systems and copies have been checked.
- Keep evidence of completed actions.
- Improve accountability when responding to customer requests.

It provides a practical starting point without requiring the company to purchase or build a large customer-data platform.

## What it cost

The workbook was created using a spreadsheet format and does not require a new server or paid hosting service.

There is no separate software subscription cost included in this proposal. Staff time will be required to maintain the register, investigate requests and review records.

The company must also provide appropriate access controls and obtain qualified privacy advice before using the tracker with real customer requests.
